# SchoenleLabs

### Intelligent technology. Human-centered solutions.

SchoenleLabs is an independent technology lab exploring how artificial intelligence, modern software architecture, and thoughtful user experience can solve complex, real-world problems.

## What We Explore

- **AI-Assisted Decision Support** — Turning fragmented information into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Intelligence** — Keeping people informed, empowered, and in control.
- **Intelligent Workflow Orchestration** — Combining generative AI with reliable, deterministic systems.
- **Privacy-Conscious Engineering** — Building security and responsible data practices into the foundation.
- **Experience-Driven Innovation** — Designing technology around the people who actually use it.

## What We're Building

We're developing an AI-assisted platform that helps people navigate complicated, information-intensive processes, from understanding their goals to organizing the steps needed to achieve them.

Our approach combines conversational AI, structured information, human validation, and outcome-driven workflow management.
